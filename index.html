परिचय · Aboutशंख की ध्वनि, कथा का अमृत

Shankha Amrit retells the legends of Kerala's great temples and the timeless stories of the Puranas — Guruvayurappan and Unnikkannan, Swami Ayyappa, Sree Muthappan, Adi Shankaracharya and more — as illustrated films and Shorts for devotees everywhere.

केरल की कथाएँ

Temple legends from Guruvayur, Sabarimala, Parassinikadavu, Vaikom, Chottanikkara and Kalady.

दो भाषाएँ

Every story is made separately in Hindi and in Malayalam, each with its own narration.

पर्व के साथ

New stories arrive with the festivals — Ekadasi, Ashtami, Mandala season and Theyyam season.
